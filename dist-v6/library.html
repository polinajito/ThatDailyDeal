Spacing

4px base scale. Each bar's width equals the token value so the relative steps are visible at a glance. Use these for padding, margin, and gap throughout.

Border radius

Discrete radius scale. Reach for --radius-pill on capsule buttons and banners, --radius-circle for avatars and icon buttons, the rest by visual weight.

Shadows

Drop shadows for elevation. --shadow-sheet casts upward (used by bottom sheets). --shadow-gold-glow is the accent glow under the primary CTA.

Liquid glass

Price pill

Liquid-glass pill at the top of each deal card showing product name, current price, and (when applicable) the strikethrough old price + discount badge. Three states: regular (just the now-price), with sale (now + old + discount), and out of stock (strikethrough now-price, muted weight).

Source: v6.css:622-658 (.price-pill, .pp-name, .pp-now, .pp-old, .pp-discount); v6.css:1124-1128 (sold-out variant). Uses tokens: --glass-bg, --glass-border, --glass-blur, --glass-shadow, --color-white, --color-red, --font-family-primary.

Button

Composable button. Pick a color (.btn-primary = gold, .btn-secondary = blue), a size (.btn-sm / .btn-md / .btn-lg), optionally a fill modifier (.btn-ghost for outlined), and optionally .btn-icon-only for square icon buttons. The .btn-glass modifier swaps the solid bg for liquid glass — designed for the action-stack pattern. Icon position is determined by DOM order (icon first = left, icon last = right).

Source patterns in prototype: v6.css:318-342 (.dd-btn), v6.css:1177-1194 (.card-cta), v6.css:575-612 (.act-btn). Tokens: --color-gold, --color-blue, --color-white, --glass-*, --radius-pill, --font-family-primary, --shadow-gold-glow.

Deals banner

The pill-shaped "Today's deals end in HH:MM:SS" countdown that sits just below the price pill on each deal card. Centered content, clock icon + label in white, the live timer in red. Hidden globally when body.no-banner is set.

Source: v6.css:136-169 (.deals-banner, .deals-banner-icon, .deals-banner-time); markup in v6.js renderCard. Tokens: --glass-bg, --glass-border, --glass-blur, --glass-shadow, --radius-pill, --color-red, --font-family-primary.

Swipe stamps

Large rotated overlays that flash on a deal-card during a swipe-up gesture, or persist on sold-out cards. (Horizontal swipes now navigate between deals and show no stamp.) Variants signal intent: green Add (swipe up on an available deal), gold Notify (swipe up on a sold-out deal), red Sold Out (persistent, centered). The red Skip variant is retained but currently unused by the gesture model.

Visual: components.css (.swipe-stamp, .stamp-add, .stamp-skip, .stamp-soldout, .stamp-notify). Visibility (.deal-card.show-add, .show-notify, .sold-out) is owned by v6.css. Tokens: --color-green, --color-red, --color-gold, --space-2, --space-4, --space-5, --radius-md.

Toast

Two transient overlays. The small tap toast is an unobtrusive black pill for one-off feedback (liked, link copied, added to cart) — fires for ~1.3s. The rich toast is a higher-stakes confirmation card with icon + title + supportive text (currently: "We'll notify you when this item is back in stock") — fires for ~2.8s.

Visual: components.css (.toast, .rich-toast, .rich-toast-icon, .rich-toast-title, .rich-toast-sub). Positioning + slide-in stays in v6.css; fired by showToast() / showRichToast() in v6.js.

Info popup

Brand-blue card centred on the screen behind a dimming backdrop, pairing the brand logo with a sentence of copy. Carries a top-right close (X) and a gold "Okay, cool" CTA (.btn.btn-primary). In the prototype it's opened by tapping the header flag ("America based, serving up deals since 2009.") and dismissed by the X, the CTA, tapping the backdrop, or pressing Escape.

Visual: components.css (.info-popup, .info-popup-logo, .info-popup-text, .info-popup-close, .info-popup-cta). Centring, backdrop, and open/close transition stay in v6.css; toggled in v6.js via the body.flag-popup-open state.

Challenge card

Full-bleed promo card that rides the deal filmstrip as an interstitial (3rd card). Brand-green gradient surface with a "Play & Win" badge, money-bag hero, headline + copy, two reward chips, and a gold "Start a Challenge" CTA (.btn.btn-primary). It only navigates horizontally — no swipe-up or tap-to-details — and the CTA shows a placeholder toast.

Visual: components.css (.challenge-card + .challenge-badge, -hero, -title, -sub, -rewards/-reward, -cta, -foot). Deck sizing (full width/height, touch-action) shared with .deal-card in v6.css; built + spliced into the sequence in v6.js (buildChallengeCard, SEQUENCE).

Challenge mini

Compact, horizontal sibling of the challenge card for list contexts like the Challenges tab. A lighter-green top band pairs the money-bag hero with an eyebrow, headline, and one-line copy; a darker-green footer band carries the two reward stats inline (amount over uppercase label) rather than as white chips. An optional .challenge-mini-cta band adds a full-width primary button (e.g. "Create Challenge") on actionable cards; omit it on non-actionable entries.

Visual: components.css (.challenge-mini + -top, -hero, -anim, -text, -eyebrow, -title, -sub, -rewards/-reward, -amount, -label, -cta). The CTA reuses .btn.btn-primary. Rendered into the Challenges tab by contests.js; the loopmoney hero loads via window.playLoopmoney (assets/loopmoney.js).

Event card

Horizontal ticket for an upcoming event. A white left stub stacks the date (month / big day / weekday); a green body carries an eyebrow row (bolt + uppercase kicker), a bold headline, and a time/tagline subline. The seam is a vertical dashed perforation flanked by two circular notches — the same ticket cut-out technique as .challenge-mini, rotated 90°. A faint gold dot and two white dots are baked into the body background as confetti.

Visual: components.css (.event-card + -date, -month, -day, -dow, -body, -eyebrow, -title, -sub). Notches/perforation reuse the masking approach from .challenge-mini; colours and type come from tokens.css.

Weekly stats

A compact two-cell counter block for "this week" usage against a cap (e.g. challenges sent / accepted, capped at one each per week). An uppercase title sits above a row of liquid-glass cells; each cell pairs a label, a big value with a muted /cap denominator, and a contextual subline — "N left" while under the cap, "resets Mon" once maxed.

Visual: components.css (.weekly-stats, -title, -row; .weekly-stat, -label, -value + .wk-cap, -sub). Rendered into the Challenges tab by contests.js. Tokens: --glass-*, --radius-lg, --color-white, type scale.

Segmented tabs

A pill-shaped switch on a liquid-glass track for two-or-more mutually exclusive views (e.g. Sent / Accepted challenge history). The active tab fills gold to match the bottom-nav selection. Mark the active .seg-tab with .is-active; the consuming view toggles which panel shows.

Visual: components.css (.seg, .seg-tab, .is-active). Tokens: --glass-*, --radius-pill, --color-gold, --shadow-gold-glow, type scale.

Match card

A compact challenge-history card: two players (initial avatar + name) either side of a "vs", then an outcome line. The winning player takes .is-winner (gold avatar ring + glow); the outcome reads .is-win (green) or .is-loss (muted), followed by a quiet topic·date subline. Liquid-glass surface; stack several in a .match-list.

Visual: components.css (.match-card, .match-players, .match-player + .is-winner, .match-avatar, .match-name, .match-vs, .match-meta, .match-outcome + .is-win/.is-loss, .match-sub, .match-list). Tokens: --glass-*, --radius-lg, --radius-circle, --color-blue, --color-gold, --color-green, type scale.

List card

Grouped liquid-glass list for settings-style screens. A .list-card stacks .list-row items separated by hairline dividers. Each row takes an optional leading .list-row-icon, a .list-row-text block (.list-row-label + optional .list-row-sub), and a trailing slot — a gold .list-row-chevron disclosure arrow or a control (reuse the .ns-switch toggle). Tappable rows are <button>; rows whose control owns interaction use .is-static. .list-row-destructive turns the label red for irreversible actions.

Visual: components.css (.list-card, .list-row, .list-row-icon, .list-row-text, .list-row-label, .list-row-sub, .list-row-chevron, .list-row-destructive). Reuses the .ns-switch toggle. Tokens: --glass-*, --radius-lg, --radius-sm, --color-white, --color-gold, --color-red, type scale.

Form field

Labelled text input for the dark glass surface (settings detail pages). A .field stacks a .field-label over a .field-input; drop .field rows inside a .list-card for a grouped form with hairline dividers. Focus draws a gold border + soft ring. A readonly input reads as a static value (no ring), for non-editable rows like "Member since".

Visual: components.css (.field, .field-label, .field-input). Reuses .list-card as the form container. Tokens: --glass-*, --radius-sm, --color-white, --color-gold, type scale.

Stepper

Compact quantity control on a liquid-glass pill — a round − button, the current value, and a round +. Disable − at the minimum (1). Used by the cart line to edit item amounts; reusable wherever a small numeric stepper is needed.

Visual: components.css (.stepper, .stepper-btn, .stepper-value). Tokens: --glass-*, --radius-pill, --radius-circle, --color-white, type scale.

Cart line

One item in the cart: a liquid-glass row with a media thumb, the product name + a remove control, a price cluster (current price + struck old price + a reused .pp-discount sale badge), a quantity .stepper, and the line total. .cart-line-tag flags a bulk-price unlock (gold pill). Stack several in a .cart-list.

Visual: components.css (.cart-line + -thumb, -body, -top, -name, -remove, -prices, -now, -old, -tag, -foot, -total; .cart-list). Reuses .pp-discount + .stepper. Tokens: --glass-*, --radius-lg, --radius-md, --color-white, --color-gold, type scale.

Cart summary

Totals panel for the cart / checkout. A stack of .cart-sum-row label/value pairs: .is-saved turns a row green for the combined discount, .is-total bumps the final row above a hairline divider. Subtotal − You saved + Shipping reconciles to Total. Usually followed by a primary .btn checkout button.

Visual: components.css (.cart-summary, .cart-sum-row + .is-saved / .is-total). Tokens: --glass-*, --radius-lg, --color-white, --color-green, type scale.

Coupon

Rewards-tab redemption ticket. The right edge is a perforated vertical stub the user taps to activate — "Activate" is part of the ticket, not a button below it (movie-ticket vocabulary). A square art panel carries the $ amount, beside a title, sub, and a gold points pill. Five visual variants — .coupon-classic (recommended ship), .coupon-burst, .coupon-foil, .coupon-pop, .coupon-stamp — and three states: locked (.is-locked, disabled stub + progress bar toward unlock), active (.is-active, green stub reading "Activated"), and used (.is-used, desaturated, for the My Rewards tab).

Visual: components.css (.coupon + -body, -art, -amount, -info, -title, -sub, -meta; .stub, .stub-text, .perf, .notch; variants .coupon-classic/-burst/-foil/-pop/-stamp + .coupon-confetti/.coupon-star; states .is-locked/.is-active/.is-used). Composes .gold-pill.points-pill and .progress. Rendered into the Rewards tab by rewards.js; positioned by rewards.css (the .screen-rewards .notch override matches the notch holes to the sheet gradient). Tokens: --color-gold, --color-gold-dark, --color-blue, --color-blue-dark, --color-blue-deep, --color-red, --color-green, --color-ink, --shadow-md, the --type-* scale. Display amount uses Fredoka (falls back to Poppins).
