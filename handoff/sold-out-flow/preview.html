Sold-Out Flow — Component Preview

Static visual reference for the four components in the out-of-stock UX: the persistent Sold Out badge, the left-swipe Skip stamp, the right-swipe Notify me stamp (replaces "Add" on sold-out cards), and the notify confirmation toast shown after the user subscribes to a back-in-stock alert. All shown at full opacity against the dark card backdrop. No iPhone frame, no app chrome, no interactivity — see spec.md for states and timing, and flutter_snippets.dart for paste-ready Flutter widgets.
